The Authentic Rainforest Experience

A river boat, the Selva Verde trails and a gondola gliding through the canopy.

Duration10 hours
DifficultyEasy
Departs6:30 to 7:30 a.m.
PickupHotels in San José

The day, in short

We cross Braulio Carrillo National Park to Sarapiquí for a boat ride on the river, then walk the private trails of Selva Verde Eco Lodge with our naturalist.

After lunch, the Rain Forest Aerial Tram carries you through the treetops on the way back. If you want more adrenaline, swap the gondola for seven zip lines.
