Irazú Volcano

A green lagoon inside the crater and, on a good morning, two oceans at once.

Duration6 hours
DifficultyEasy
Departs6:30 to 7:30 a.m.
PickupHotels in San José

The day, in short

The road climbs from coffee farms to fern forest and finally to bare volcanic ground at the summit of Irazú, where a lookout faces the crater and its green lagoon.

We come down through Cartago to visit the Basilica of the Virgin of the Angels and are back in San José early in the afternoon.
