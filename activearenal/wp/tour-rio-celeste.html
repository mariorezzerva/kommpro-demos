The day, in short

Deep inside Tenorio Volcano National Park, two ordinary rivers meet and turn a surreal shade of turquoise, a natural phenomenon found in only a handful of places on Earth.

On this full-day escape you'll hike through pristine rainforest to the legendary Río Celeste Waterfall, pass steaming volcanic vents and bubbling blue lagoons, and learn the science and folklore behind the color from your naturalist guide. It's nature, photography, and pure wonder in one unforgettable day.
